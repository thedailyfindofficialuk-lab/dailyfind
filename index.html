The Daily Find Studio
News carousels for TikTok

From trusted news to a finished TikTok carousel

The Daily Find Studio is a desktop tool for news creators. It gathers stories from reputable publishers, groups reports about the same event, checks key facts across outlets, and prepares a four-slide photo carousel with a caption and hashtags.

How it works

  1. Today's Stories: fresh stories from trusted RSS feeds, ranked by freshness, coverage and audience interest.
  2. Create: each story is researched automatically. Every slide is based on a sentence from a publisher's article, with sources listed.
  3. Review: the creator reads the slides and any fact-check notes, edits the wording and approves the carousel.
  4. Share to TikTok: with the creator's permission, the carousel is sent to their TikTok account using TikTok's official Content Posting API. The creator chooses who can see it, whether comments are allowed, and confirms TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation before anything is posted.

Using TikTok with The Daily Find Studio

The Daily Find Studio only connects to a TikTok account after the account owner signs in with TikTok and grants permission. It uses that permission solely to show the account name and to post content the creator has explicitly chosen to share. It never posts automatically or without a final confirmation from the creator.
