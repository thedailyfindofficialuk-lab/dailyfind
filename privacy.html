Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Who we are

The Daily Find Studio ("the app") is operated by The Daily Find UK. It is a desktop application that runs on the creator's own computer.

What information the app uses

  • TikTok account information: when you connect TikTok, the app receives an access token, your TikTok open ID, and your display name and avatar, so it can show which account content will be posted to.
  • Posting settings: creator information from TikTok (such as available privacy options and whether comments are allowed), which is used only to build the posting screen.
  • Content you choose to post: the slide images and caption you approve.

How information is stored

Access tokens and settings are stored only on your own computer, inside the app's data folder. They are not sent to The Daily Find UK or any third party, except to TikTok when the app calls TikTok's API on your behalf.

To let TikTok collect the images for a post, the app briefly publishes the approved slide images to a public web address controlled by the creator. The images are removed once TikTok has received them.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, share or rent personal information.
  • We do not post anything without your explicit confirmation on each post.
  • We do not read your messages, followers or other private TikTok data.

Disconnecting

You can disconnect TikTok at any time from the app's Advanced page, which deletes the stored tokens from your computer. You can also remove the app's access in your TikTok settings under Security > Manage app permissions.

Contact
